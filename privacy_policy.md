PantryPal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

PantryPal is designed to help users manage household food inventories and shopping lists.

Information We Collect

PantryPal does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Data Stored on Your Device

The application stores inventory data, shopping lists, barcode information, and user-entered product information locally on your device.

Camera Access

PantryPal uses the device camera solely for barcode scanning. Images are not stored or transmitted.

CSV Import and Export

Users may export inventory data to CSV files and import CSV files into the application. These files are stored only on the user's device unless the user chooses to share them.

Third-Party Services

PantryPal does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or cloud storage services.

Data Sharing

PantryPal does not sell, share, or disclose user data to third parties.
